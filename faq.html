Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers before you book.

How do I get a quote?

Use the quote calculator on the booking page for an instant estimate, or message us on 561-574-0921. We confirm every quote before your first visit.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many clients give us entry instructions and relax. If you prefer to be there, that is fine too.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes. We arrive with professional-grade products and tools. If you prefer specific products, tell us in your booking notes.

Can I set up recurring cleaning?

Yes. Choose weekly, bi-weekly or monthly plans and save on every visit. Deep, move-in/out and post-construction cleans are one-time services.

What is your 100% satisfaction guarantee?

If anything is missed, tell us and we will make it right. Your satisfaction is our priority.

Which areas do you serve?

We proudly serve Palm Beach County, FL, including West Palm Beach and nearby communities. See the areas page for the list.

How do I reschedule or cancel?

Call or message us as early as you can. We will find a new time that works around your schedule.

Do you clean offices and commercial buildings?

Yes. Our janitorial service covers offices, buildings and facilities on a daily, weekly or custom schedule.

Still have a question?

Tell us about your space and get an instant estimate. We reply quickly with honest, affordable quotes.
